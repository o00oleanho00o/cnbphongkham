# PROJECT CONTEXT — PEMA

## Business context

Pema is a real dermatology/aesthetic clinic in Vietnam.

Dr. Tâm, the clinic owner, has expressed interest in collaborating:
- build the system together first,
- use Pema as the real environment,
- later commercialize the product.

The software/AI side has strong engineering capability.

## Current observation

Existing clinic software can look very complete but clinic staff still complain:
- missing useful features,
- poor understanding of clinic workflow,
- operational mismatch.

This is the central product hypothesis to investigate.

One current/relevant system:
https://annam.vttechsolution.vn/

Do not assume VTTech is bad.
Study what it does well and where the gap may actually come from:
- workflow fit,
- usability,
- customization,
- specialization,
- training,
- implementation,
- fragmented data,
- patient journey,
- multi-session treatment,
- follow-up,
- clinical photography.

## Product ambition

Start from Pema.

Do not immediately build for every clinic.

Use Pema to discover:
- real workflows,
- high-frequency actions,
- data model,
- pain points,
- useful management indicators,
- patient experience,
- clinical dataset structure.

Then identify the reusable core for commercialization.

### AI

Do not lead with AI diagnosis.

Early practical AI:
- pre-visit brief
- medical note draft
- timeline summary
- follow-up detection
- missing-data detection
- image progress assistance
- natural-language analytics

## Strategic idea

The long-term moat may not be the CRUD software.

It may come from:
- deeply correct workflow,
- longitudinal patient data,
- structured treatment outcomes,
- clinical photography,
- patient engagement,
- years of high-quality Pema data,
- workflow-aware AI.

Research whether this thesis is valid.
