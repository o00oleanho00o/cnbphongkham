From Houston Nail Salons to 20,000+ Clients Worldwide Vuong Pham Founder & CEO of Fastboy Marketing In 2010, Vuong Pham started Fastboy Marketing with a simple mission: help small businesses get found online and grow. His first clients were nail salons in Houston, Texas — businesses with incredible talent but no online presence. Vuong spent years mastering every aspect of digital marketing — SEO, Google Ads, Facebook advertising, social media, and web design — and applying those skills to help small business owners compete in an increasingly digital world. Word spread fast. What started as a handful of local nail salons quickly grew into a full-service agency serving restaurants, barbershops, auto shops, coffee shops, and businesses across dozens of industries. Today, Fastboy Marketing is one of the most trusted marketing partners for small businesses in the US, Canada, Australia, and Europe — with over 20,000 clients and a team of 200+ specialists.
